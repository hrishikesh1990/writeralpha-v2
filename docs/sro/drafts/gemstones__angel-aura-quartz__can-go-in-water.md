# Can Angel Aura Quartz Go in Water?

Angel Aura Quartz has two components with different water tolerances. The quartz substrate (SiO2, Mohs 7) is chemically stable and water-safe. The iridescent coating — vapor-deposited platinum and silver — is softer and can be dulled or weakened by prolonged soaking, salt water, and abrasive contact. A brief lukewarm rinse is generally safe; soaking and salt water are not.

## Why the Coating Is the Issue, Not the Quartz

Angel Aura Quartz is created by placing natural clear quartz in a vacuum chamber and bonding vaporized metals — typically platinum and silver — to the crystal surface at high temperature. The result is a permanent but thin film responsible for the rainbow iridescence. The film is measured in nanometers and follows the surface geometry of the underlying quartz.

The coating metals are soft relative to the quartz beneath them. Platinum rates roughly 3.5–4 on the Mohs scale and silver roughly 2.5–3 — meaning the iridescent layer can be scratched by everyday grit that the quartz substrate would resist completely. In water, the risk is compounded: water carries dissolved minerals and particulates that act as fine abrasives during any movement of the stone; prolonged soaking can also work moisture into micro-gaps at the coating-quartz interface and accelerate edge wear.

Salt water is more aggressive than plain water. Salt itself is abrasive at the grain level, and in solution it can interact with the silver component of the coating. Most crystal-care references specific to Angel Aura Quartz advise avoiding salt water and saltwater baths entirely. For the broader question of which crystals are salt-safe, see [salt-safe crystals](/learn/salt-safe-crystals).

## What Brief Water Contact Does and Does Not Do

A brief rinse — a few seconds under cool or lukewarm running water — will not strip or visibly damage an intact Angel Aura Quartz coating. Many collectors use this as a quick physical clean for dust and fingerprints. The key conditions are:

- Water temperature is cool to lukewarm, not hot (thermal shock risks the bond)
- Contact time is short (seconds, not minutes)
- No salt, soap with harsh chemicals, or abrasive material is present
- The piece is patted dry immediately with a soft cloth and not left to air-dry while wet
- No scrubbing or rubbing

Soaking — leaving the piece submerged for minutes or longer — is not recommended. Neither is using the stone in a bath, fountain, or any setup where it sits in water repeatedly.

## Sun Exposure After Water Contact

Some sources indicate that Angel Aura Quartz should not be left in extended direct sunlight, as UV exposure may affect the iridescent finish over time. Dry in shade or indirect light rather than placing in strong sun. For general guidance on sun exposure, see [sun-safe crystals](/learn/sun-safe-crystals).

## Cleaning Without Water

Dry cleaning methods are lower-risk for the coating than any water contact. A soft dry microfiber cloth handles fingerprints and light dust without exposing the finish to moisture. For energetic cleansing without physical contact, smoke, sound, and selenite are the standard alternatives — all covered on the [how to cleanse Angel Aura Quartz](/gemstones/angel-aura-quartz/how-to-cleanse) page. For the general framework of water-safe stones, see [what crystals can go in water](/learn/what-crystals-can-go-in-water).
