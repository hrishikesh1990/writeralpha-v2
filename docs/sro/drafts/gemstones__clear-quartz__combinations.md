# Clear Quartz Combinations — Which Crystals Pair Well

Clear quartz is traditionally described as an amplifier — in crystal practice, it is believed to intensify the properties of any stone placed near it or used alongside it. This makes it the most broadly compatible crystal for combination work. The pairings below are organized by goal; all associations are traditional, not scientifically established. For clear quartz's own associations, see [clear quartz meaning](/gemstones/clear-quartz/meaning).

## How Clear Quartz Works in Combination

Because clear quartz carries no strong directional association of its own (unlike, say, [black tourmaline](/gemstones/black-tourmaline) for grounding or [rose quartz](/gemstones/rose-quartz) for love), practitioners use it as a multiplier. A common approach is to place clear quartz points facing outward from a central stone in a crystal grid, or to hold a piece of clear quartz alongside a stone with a specific intention. For grid design, see [crystal grids](/learn/crystal-grids).

The combinations below are organized by what practitioners are working toward:

## For Healing and Wellbeing

**Clear quartz + [amethyst](/gemstones/amethyst).** One of the most widely used pairings in crystal practice. Amethyst is traditionally associated with calm, spiritual awareness, and support during stress. Combined with clear quartz's amplifying quality, this pairing is used by practitioners seeking a combination of clarity and calm. Both stones are physically compatible — quartz at Mohs 7, amethyst (also quartz) at Mohs 7.

**Clear quartz + [rose quartz](/gemstones/rose-quartz).** Rose quartz is traditionally associated with self-love, emotional healing, and compassion. Pairing it with clear quartz is believed to amplify these qualities. This is a common combination for practitioners working on emotional wellbeing. Both are quartz varieties and are equally durable.

**Clear quartz + [selenite](/gemstones/selenite).** Selenite (Mohs 2) is traditionally associated with cleansing and clarity. Clear quartz alongside selenite is used in practice for spaces intended to feel clear and elevated. Note the hardness mismatch — do not store them in contact, as the quartz will scratch the selenite. This pairing appears frequently in [crystals for healing](/crystals/crystals-for-healing).

## For Focus and Concentration

**Clear quartz + [citrine](/gemstones/citrine).** Citrine is traditionally associated with mental energy, optimism, and clarity of thought. Paired with clear quartz, this combination is used by practitioners working on focus, motivation, or creative output. Both are quartz varieties (citrine is heat-treated or naturally occurring yellow quartz) and are physically compatible. See also [crystals for concentration](/crystals/crystals-for-concentration).

**Clear quartz + [labradorite](/gemstones/labradorite).** Labradorite (Mohs 6–6.5) is traditionally associated with intuition, analytical thinking, and protection during mentally demanding work. Combined with clear quartz's amplifying quality, this pairing is used when practitioners want heightened perceptual clarity alongside focused thinking.

## For Protection and Grounding

**Clear quartz + [black tourmaline](/gemstones/black-tourmaline).** Black tourmaline is the most commonly used grounding and protective stone in crystal practice. Clear quartz alongside it is believed to amplify its protective qualities and add clarity to the grounding. This is one of the most frequently recommended combination pairings. Tourmaline (Mohs 7–7.5) and quartz are physically compatible.

**Clear quartz + [obsidian](/gemstones/obsidian).** Obsidian (volcanic glass, Mohs ~5.5) is traditionally associated with truth, shadow work, and grounding. Pairing it with clear quartz is said to bring clarity to what obsidian reveals. Handle obsidian with care, particularly raw pieces — the edges can be very sharp.

## For Abundance and Intention

**Clear quartz + [citrine](/gemstones/citrine).** Also appears here: citrine is a traditional abundance stone, and clear quartz is believed to amplify any stated intention. This is a common pairing for manifesting work.

**Clear quartz + [aventurine](/gemstones/aventurine).** [Green aventurine](/gemstones/aventurine) is traditionally associated with luck and opportunity. Paired with clear quartz, it is used in combination for intention work around growth and new beginnings.

## Pairings Practitioners Approach With Care

Most crystal traditions do not identify combinations that cause physical harm — stones do not chemically interact simply by being near each other. However, some practitioners advise against combining:

- **Two strong amplifying stones** without a grounding stone to balance. For example, clear quartz alongside moldavite (a strong amplifier in many traditions) is described by some as potentially overwhelming, and practitioners often recommend adding a grounding stone such as black tourmaline.
- **Clear quartz in a bedroom** alongside multiple other stimulating stones, if sleep is a priority. Practitioners suggest keeping amplifying combinations for daytime use.

These are convention-based cautions within the tradition, not physical risks.

## Using Clear Quartz in Grids and Sets

Clear quartz points are the most commonly used stones for the outer points of a crystal grid, regardless of the central stone. Their traditional amplifying and projecting quality makes them a natural complement to almost any other stone. For a full guide to building crystal grids, see [crystal grids](/learn/crystal-grids).
