# Citrine Affirmations — What to Say and How to Use Them

Citrine affirmations are short, present-tense statements aligned with the stone's traditional associations: abundance, confidence, creativity, and joy. Practitioners hold or wear the stone while speaking or thinking the affirmation, using it as a focal point for intention-setting. The affirmations below are organized by theme to match what you are working toward.

## Why Citrine for Affirmations

Citrine is traditionally associated with the solar plexus chakra (Manipura) — the energy center linked to personal power, self-worth, and motivated action. Its fire element and Sun associations make it a common choice for practitioners working on confidence, prosperity mindset, and creative output. For the full account of citrine's traditional meaning, see [citrine meaning](/gemstones/citrine/meaning).

Holding a physical object while speaking an affirmation adds a sensory anchor to the practice. Crystal practice describes citrine as a stone that "holds" the intention, making it useful for repeating a practice over days or weeks.

## Affirmations by Theme

### Abundance and prosperity

These affirmations draw on citrine's "merchant's stone" tradition and its link to material and energetic abundance:

- "I am open to abundance in all its forms."
- "Money flows to me easily and I use it wisely."
- "I attract opportunities that align with my goals."
- "I deserve to prosper and I welcome it."
- "Abundance is my natural state."

Citrine's prosperity associations are explored in [crystals for money](/crystals/crystals-for-money).

### Confidence and self-worth

These affirmations address the solar plexus energy center and citrine's traditional link to personal power:

- "I trust myself and my decisions."
- "I am capable, worthy, and enough."
- "I speak and act from a place of confidence."
- "My confidence grows every day."
- "I stand in my own power."

See [crystals for confidence](/crystals/crystals-for-confidence) for how citrine fits the broader confidence stone framework.

### Creativity and motivation

These suit writers, artists, entrepreneurs, and anyone in a period of creative work:

- "My creativity flows freely and abundantly."
- "I am full of ideas and the energy to bring them to life."
- "I take action on what matters to me."
- "My work has value and I share it with confidence."
- "I am inspired and inspiring."

### Joy and positivity

These draw on citrine's traditional association with sunshine, warmth, and a lifted mood:

- "I choose joy today."
- "I radiate warmth and positivity."
- "Good things come to me and I am grateful."
- "I release what no longer serves me and welcome what does."
- "My life is full of things worth celebrating."

## How to Use Citrine Affirmations

**A simple daily practice:**

1. Hold a cleansed citrine piece in both hands — a tumbled stone or a palm stone works best for this, as it can be held comfortably. For cleansing the stone first, see [how to cleanse citrine](/gemstones/citrine/how-to-cleanse).
2. Take three slow breaths to settle your attention.
3. Speak your chosen affirmation aloud, or repeat it internally, three times. Slow and deliberate repetition is described as more effective than rapid cycling.
4. Hold the stone for a further 30 to 60 seconds in silence, letting the statement sit.
5. Set the stone in a visible place — on your desk, on your windowsill, in your pocket — as a physical reminder through the day.

This practice takes under five minutes. Many practitioners do it in the morning, using the stone as a daily anchor for the intention they are working with.

**During meditation:** Place citrine on the solar plexus area (below the ribcage, above the navel) while lying down and repeat affirmations internally. This is a common approach in chakra meditation practice.

**Journaling alongside affirmations:** Write the affirmation at the top of a journal page, then write freely for five to ten minutes on what it means for you today. The citrine can be held or placed near the writing. This combination of affirmation and reflective writing is described by practitioners as deepening the intention.

## Pairing Stones with Affirmations

If you work with multiple stones, consider what each traditionally adds:

- **Citrine + clear quartz:** Amplification of the affirmation's intent.
- **Citrine + pyrite:** Grounding the abundance affirmation in practical action energy.
- **Citrine + rose quartz:** Softening confidence affirmations with self-compassion.
