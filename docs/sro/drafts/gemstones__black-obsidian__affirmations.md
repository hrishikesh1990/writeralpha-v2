# Black Obsidian Affirmations and How to Use Them

Black obsidian is traditionally associated with protection, grounding, truth, and the release of what no longer serves. Affirmations used with black obsidian in crystal practice are typically short, present-tense statements that align with these associations. The practice is a form of intention-setting, not a medical or psychological treatment.

## Affirmations Suited to Black Obsidian

The following examples reflect the traditional associations most commonly linked to black obsidian in crystal communities. Choose the ones that feel relevant to your situation, or adapt them in your own words.

**Protection and boundaries**
- "I am protected and safe."
- "I release what is not mine to carry."
- "My energy is my own."

**Grounding and stability**
- "I am rooted and present."
- "I stand firmly in who I am."
- "I am connected to the earth beneath me."

**Truth and self-honesty**
- "I see myself clearly and with compassion."
- "I welcome the truth, even when it is difficult."
- "I release patterns that no longer serve my growth."

**Releasing and letting go**
- "I let go of what I cannot change."
- "I release fear and move forward with clarity."
- "I am free from the weight of the past."

## How to Use Affirmations with Black Obsidian

**Step 1 — Choose or write your affirmation.** Select one that fits your current intention. Specific affirmations tend to work better in practice than very general ones. Use the present tense ("I am", "I release") rather than future tense ("I will be").

**Step 2 — Prepare the stone.** Hold the black obsidian in your hands for a moment to warm it. If you have recently cleansed it, proceed directly. If not, consider a brief cleansing first — see [how to cleanse Black Obsidian](/gemstones/black-obsidian/how-to-cleanse).

**Step 3 — Settle and breathe.** Find a quiet place where you can sit without interruption for a few minutes. Take two or three slow breaths.

**Step 4 — State the affirmation.** Speak the affirmation aloud or say it silently while holding the stone. Repeat it three to five times, or as many times as feels natural. Some practitioners find it helpful to visualize the meaning of the statement as they say it.

**Step 5 — Close the practice.** After repeating the affirmation, sit quietly for a moment. Place the stone in its usual spot — on a surface near your workspace, by the bed, or in a pocket — as a physical reminder of the intention you set.

**Timing**: Many practitioners use affirmations with black obsidian in the morning as a grounding practice, or in the evening to set an intention for sleep. There is no required timing; choose what fits your routine.

## Writing Your Own Affirmations

Any affirmation that aligns with black obsidian's traditional associations — protection, grounding, truth, release — is appropriate. A few guidelines from crystal practice:

- Use the present tense. "I am grounded" rather than "I will be grounded."
- Keep statements positive and specific. "I release tension from my day" rather than "I am not anxious."
- Keep them short enough to remember easily.

## Using Affirmations with Paired Stones

Some practitioners use affirmations while holding both black obsidian and a paired stone. For example, holding obsidian in one hand for protection and a piece of rose quartz in the other for emotional gentleness during affirmations focused on difficult self-reflection.
