# Who Should Be Cautious About Wearing Hematite

Hematite is physically safe for most adults when worn as jewelry or carried as a tumbled stone. The main situations that warrant caution are those involving magnetically sensitive medical devices, those who handle damaged specimens regularly, and people who follow astrological traditions that advise against it for specific chart configurations.

## Physical and Medical Cautions

**"Magnetic hematite"** sold in many gift, crystal, and tourist shops is not genuine hematite. It is a man-made material — typically sintered barium ferrite or strontium ferrite — that is strongly magnetic. People with pacemakers, implanted cardiac defibrillators, cochlear implants, or other magnetically sensitive medical devices should avoid "magnetic hematite" and check with their physician before wearing any strongly magnetic material close to those devices. Genuine hematite (Fe₂O₃) is not strongly magnetic and does not carry this specific risk; however, confirming which material you actually have is worthwhile before prolonged wear near implanted devices.

Hematite dust is an irritant. People who cut, grind, carve, or break hematite specimens — lapidary workers, sculptors, or collectors doing restoration work — should wear appropriate respiratory protection when producing dust. Casual handling of polished tumbled stones, beads, or jewelry does not generate significant dust and poses no comparable risk. Wash hands after handling any rough or freshly broken mineral material.

Small hematite beads, tumbled stones, and chip pieces are a choking hazard for young children. Keep loose pieces out of reach of children under three.

## Astrological and Energetic Cautions

Hematite is traditionally linked to the planet Mars and is associated with Aries and Aquarius in Western crystal astrology — see [hematite meaning](/gemstones/hematite/meaning) for the full traditional profile. Some Vedic astrology practitioners advise caution for people whose birth charts show a strongly placed or afflicted Mars, reasoning that adding a Mars-ruled stone could intensify those chart qualities rather than balance them. This is a matter of tradition, not a verifiable outcome. Anyone for whom this is relevant should consult a practitioner familiar with their chart.

Some crystal practitioners suggest that people in an overstimulated, anxious, or depleted state approach hematite carefully, noting that its traditionally grounding character can feel heavy or draining when someone is already running low on energy. This perspective is not universal in crystal tradition and has no physical basis.

## Who Benefits Most from Hematite

For people without the cautions above, hematite is one of the most widely worn protective and grounding stones. It is durable at Mohs 5 to 6.5, resistant to everyday wear, and readily available in polished jewelry, tumbled stones, and beads at accessible prices. Hematite's role in [grounding](/crystals/crystals-for-grounding) and [protection](/crystals/crystals-for-protection) practice makes it a common choice for people who work in demanding environments.
