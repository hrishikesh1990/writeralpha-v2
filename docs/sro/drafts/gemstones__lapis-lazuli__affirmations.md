# Lapis Lazuli Affirmations: Phrases That Fit the Stone

Lapis lazuli is traditionally associated with wisdom, honest communication, and inner truth. Affirmations used with lapis lazuli draw on those themes: they are phrases focused on clarity of thought, the courage to speak honestly, and trust in one's own understanding. The practice is personal and complementary — crystals do not replace professional support.

## Why Tradition Pairs Lapis with These Themes

Lapis lazuli has been associated with wisdom and truth across cultures for over 6,500 years. In contemporary crystal practice, it is linked to the throat chakra (communication and honest expression) and the third-eye chakra (intuition and clear thought). Affirmations aligned with those themes fit the stone's established traditional associations.

## How to Use Affirmations with Lapis Lazuli

Hold the stone in your non-dominant hand or place it in front of you at table height. Take two or three slow breaths to settle your attention. Say or think each affirmation slowly, once, pausing after each one. Notice whether the phrase feels true or aspirational — both responses are useful. End by setting the stone down deliberately.

Sessions can last two minutes or twenty. Frequency matters more than duration; a daily two-minute practice with a single affirmation is more consistent than an occasional long session.

## Affirmations for Communication and Honest Expression

Use these when the focus is on speaking clearly, choosing words carefully, or overcoming reluctance to say what you mean.

- "I speak my truth with clarity and calm."
- "My words are honest, considered, and kind."
- "I express what I need without apology."
- "I communicate clearly and am understood."
- "I listen as well as I speak."

## Affirmations for Wisdom and Inner Clarity

Use these when the focus is on thinking clearly, making a decision, or trusting your own understanding.

- "My mind is clear and open to what is true."
- "I trust my own understanding and act from it."
- "Wisdom is available to me when I pause and listen."
- "I think clearly and choose well."
- "I learn steadily from what I have experienced."

## Affirmations for Inner Peace and Settled Thought

Use these when the focus is on calming mental noise or settling an overactive mind.

- "I am steady, clear, and at peace within myself."
- "My thoughts are mine to direct."
- "I release what is not mine to carry."
- "I return to stillness whenever I choose."

## Bedtime and Sleep Affirmations

Lapis lazuli is placed near the bed by practitioners who use it in the tradition that associates the stone with reflective dreams and overnight clarity.

- "My mind settles and rests."
- "I sleep peacefully and wake with clarity."
- "I trust what comes to me in the quiet."

## Writing Your Own Affirmations

Effective affirmations are stated in the present tense, describe an internal state rather than an external outcome, and are specific enough to be meaningful. "I speak clearly in difficult conversations" is more useful than "I am good at communication." Review the stone's traditional associations on the [lapis lazuli meaning page](/gemstones/lapis-lazuli/meaning) and draft a phrase that fits your own situation and intention.
