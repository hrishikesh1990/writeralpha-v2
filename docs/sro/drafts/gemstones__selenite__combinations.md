# Selenite Crystal Combinations — Pairings by Goal

Selenite is traditionally described as a cleansing and amplifying stone, which makes it a common anchor in crystal combinations. All pairing associations below are tradition — crystal practice is not supported by scientific evidence for energetic effects. Physical compatibility (which stones can be stored near selenite) is a real consideration and is covered separately.

## Criteria for Evaluating Pairings

In crystal tradition, a good pairing meets three informal tests: the stones' associated qualities complement rather than duplicate each other; the combination serves a defined intention; and the stones are physically compatible (no risk of one scratching or damaging the other during storage).

Selenite's traditional associations — clarity, calm, Moon energy, cleansing — combine most naturally with stones that are either grounding (to balance selenite's airy quality) or resonant (amplifying a shared theme). A pairing where both stones carry identical associations adds little in traditional practice.

## Pairings by Goal

### Protection and grounding

**Selenite + [Black tourmaline](/gemstones/black-tourmaline)**
In tradition this is one of the most commonly recommended combinations. Black tourmaline is associated with grounding and protective energy; selenite, with clarity and cleansing. Practitioners place both at room boundaries, entry points, or together in a grid to create what they describe as a cleansed, protected space. Physical note: black tourmaline (Mohs 7–7.5) is significantly harder than selenite and will scratch it on contact; store them separately.

**Selenite + [Black obsidian](/gemstones/black-obsidian)**
Obsidian is traditionally described as a stone of truth, grounding, and protection. Paired with selenite's clarity, practitioners use this combination for honest self-reflection and releasing what no longer serves. Physical note: obsidian (Mohs 5–5.5) will scratch selenite; keep separated in storage.

### Calm and anxiety relief

**Selenite + [Amethyst](/gemstones/amethyst)**
Amethyst is widely used in crystal practice for calm, stress relief, and sleep. Combined with selenite's clearing energy, practitioners describe this pairing for meditation, anxiety management, and pre-sleep routines. See [crystals for anxiety](/crystals/crystals-for-anxiety) and [crystals for meditation](/crystals/crystals-for-meditation). Physical note: amethyst (Mohs 7) is much harder than selenite; store separately.

**Selenite + [Lepidolite](/gemstones/lepidolite)**
Lepidolite contains lithium in its structure and is traditionally associated with emotional calm and transition. Paired with selenite's clarity, this combination is used for times of stress or emotional flux. Physical note: lepidolite (Mohs 2.5–3) is slightly harder than selenite and should not be stored touching it.

### Intuition and spiritual clarity

**Selenite + [Labradorite](/gemstones/labradorite)**
Labradorite is a feldspar associated in tradition with intuition, inner knowing, and transformation. Combined with selenite's lunar clarity, practitioners use this pairing during meditation or divination practices. Physical note: labradorite (Mohs 6–6.5) is much harder than selenite; store separately.

**Selenite + [Moonstone](/gemstones/moonstone)**
Moonstone is another stone linked to lunar energy in crystal tradition. Pairing two moon-linked stones is used by practitioners for heightening lunar connection, intuition, and cyclical awareness. Physical note: moonstone (Mohs 6–6.5) is harder than selenite; store separately.

### Amplification and clarity

**Selenite + [Clear quartz](/gemstones/clear-quartz)**
Clear quartz is described in crystal tradition as a universal amplifier. Placing it near selenite is believed by practitioners to amplify selenite's clearing qualities. Selenite slabs are often used as platforms to hold clear quartz points. Physical note: quartz (Mohs 7) will scratch selenite; when using a slab as a platform, set pieces on the slab rather than dragging them across the surface.

### Emotional warmth and self-compassion

**Selenite + [Rose quartz](/gemstones/rose-quartz)**
Rose quartz is the primary crystal associated with love and compassion in tradition. Combined with selenite's clarity, practitioners use this pairing for self-compassion work and clearing emotional confusion. Physical note: rose quartz (Mohs 7) is much harder than selenite; store separately.

**Selenite + [Fluorite](/gemstones/fluorite)**
Fluorite is associated in tradition with mental focus, clarity, and decision-making. Paired with selenite, practitioners use it for study, mental clarity rituals, and releasing confusion. Physical note: fluorite (Mohs 4) is harder than selenite and will scratch it; store separately.

**Practical advice:** Because selenite is Mohs 2.0, nearly all other crystals in common use will scratch it if stored in contact. Keep selenite in its own cloth pouch or tray rather than a mixed crystal bowl. Place it flat on a cloth or foam surface rather than on bare stone or wooden shelves.

## Pairings to Approach With Thought

There are no traditional "forbidden" combinations with selenite, but practitioners sometimes note that pairing selenite with two or more highly activating stones (citrine, carnelian) in a single sleep or meditation space can feel energetically busy for sensitive individuals. This is a personal preference consideration, not a physical risk.
