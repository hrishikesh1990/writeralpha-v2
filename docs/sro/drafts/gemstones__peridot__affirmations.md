# Peridot Affirmations — Phrases and How to Use Them

Affirmations used with peridot draw on the stone's traditional associations: compassion, emotional renewal, release of past hurt, and clarity. The practice involves holding or sitting near the stone while stating or silently repeating a chosen phrase, using the stone as a physical anchor for the intention. The effect is that of a mindfulness or intention-setting practice; it is not a clinically tested therapeutic outcome.

## Which Associations the Affirmations Draw On

Peridot is traditionally described as the Stone of Compassion and linked to emotional balance, forgiveness, and growth. In various crystal traditions it is associated with the Sun and the Earth element, with Leo and Virgo in astrological contexts, and with the heart and solar-plexus energy centers. Practitioners use affirmations that reflect these themes: releasing resentment, opening to compassion, accepting change, and cultivating confidence.

For a full explanation of where these associations come from and their historical context, see [peridot meaning](/gemstones/peridot/meaning).

## Affirmations for Compassion and Forgiveness

Practitioners use the following phrases when the intention is releasing emotional hurt, cultivating self-compassion, or practicing forgiveness:

- "I release what no longer serves my heart."
- "I am open to giving and receiving compassion."
- "I forgive myself and others with ease."
- "My heart is clear and my mind is at peace."
- "I let go of resentment and welcome renewal."

## Affirmations for Confidence and Growth

Practitioners use these phrases when the intention is building confidence, embracing personal growth, or moving forward after difficulty:

- "I grow through every experience with grace."
- "I am clear, confident, and capable."
- "I welcome new beginnings with an open heart."
- "I have the strength and clarity to move forward."
- "I am aligned with my own growth and well-being."

## Affirmations for Emotional Balance

Peridot affirmations for emotional balance suit periods of difficulty, grief, or overload. Crystals are a complementary practice; they are not a substitute for professional mental health support.

- "I am steady and grounded within myself."
- "I process my emotions with patience and care."
- "I return to balance, breath by breath."
- "I honor my feelings and release what is heavy."

## How to Use Affirmations with Peridot

The following steps describe a simple practice used in crystal tradition. It requires no special tools beyond the stone.

1. **Cleanse the stone first.** A quick rinse in warm water or a pass through smoke removes any surface energy the stone may be carrying. See [how to cleanse peridot](/gemstones/peridot/how-to-cleanse) if needed.

2. **Choose one phrase.** Select a single affirmation that matches your current intention. Using too many at once dilutes the focus of the practice.

3. **Hold the stone in both hands.** Sit comfortably, with the peridot resting in your cupped hands or held between thumb and forefinger.

4. **Breathe slowly and repeat the phrase.** Speak the affirmation aloud or silently, three to ten times, at whatever pace feels natural. Many practitioners synchronize it with the breath — saying the phrase on the exhale.

5. **Close the session.** Set the stone down deliberately. Some practitioners thank the stone; others simply place it on their altar, desk, or display space.

The whole practice takes two to five minutes. Some people work with a single affirmation for a week or more; others choose a new one each day.
