# Crazy Lace Agate Affirmations and How to Use Them

Crazy Lace Agate is associated in crystal tradition with joy, lightheartedness, and optimism — qualities the practice calls on when choosing affirmations to use alongside this stone. Affirmations suited to Crazy Lace Agate center on welcoming joy, releasing self-criticism, and approaching daily life with a lighter attitude. The stone is held in the hand or kept nearby while repeating an affirmation aloud or silently, as an intention-setting practice.

## What Makes an Affirmation Suit This Stone

In crystal tradition, affirmations are chosen to reflect or reinforce the stone's associated qualities. Crazy Lace Agate is known as the "Laughter Stone" — it is linked to solar plexus energy (confidence and personal vitality in many traditions), emotional lightness, and the ability to find joy even in difficult circumstances.

Affirmations suited to it are:
- Present tense and personal ("I am," "I welcome")
- Oriented toward joy, ease, laughter, self-acceptance, and optimism
- Short enough to hold in mind clearly

Affirmations asking for outcomes beyond the stone's traditional scope — such as grief processing, profound stillness, or deep physical healing — belong more naturally with stones traditionally associated with those qualities. See [Crazy Lace Agate meaning](/gemstones/crazy-lace-agate/meaning) for the associations this stone draws on.

## Affirmation Examples

The following are examples organized by theme. Choose one that fits your current intention, or adapt the wording to feel natural.

**Joy and lightness**
- "I welcome joy into my day."
- "I give myself permission to feel light and cheerful."
- "Laughter and ease are available to me."
- "I find something to smile about in each moment."

**Self-acceptance and emotional ease**
- "I accept myself with warmth and kindness."
- "I release the need to be perfect and choose to be at ease."
- "I am enough, and I am allowed to enjoy being here."

**Optimism and positive outlook**
- "I approach today with an open and optimistic mind."
- "Good things have room to enter my life."
- "I choose to see what is working."

**Confidence and solar plexus energy**
- "I trust my own judgment and move forward with confidence."
- "I am capable and I approach challenges with lightness."

## How to Use Affirmations With the Stone

**Basic practice:**

1. Hold a tumbled Crazy Lace Agate in the non-dominant hand (or both hands).
2. Take two or three slow, deliberate breaths to settle your attention.
3. Repeat your chosen affirmation aloud or silently, three to five times. Speak slowly and allow each word to land.
4. Rest with the stone for a moment after the final repetition before setting it down.

This process typically takes two to three minutes and can be done at any point in the day.

**Morning intention setting:** Begin the day by holding the stone and speaking one joy-focused affirmation before starting other activities. Some practitioners place the stone on a windowsill or desk afterward as a visible reminder of the intention.

**Bedtime winding down:** Hold the stone while repeating a self-acceptance or ease-focused affirmation as part of a wind-down routine. This is how some practitioners use Crazy Lace Agate in the evening, drawing on its traditionally uplifting associations to shift mood before sleep.

**Writing practice:** Some practitioners write an affirmation on paper while holding or touching the stone, treating the combination of physical writing and the stone's presence as a way to reinforce intention.
