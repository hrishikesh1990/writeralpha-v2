# Larimar Affirmations — What to Say and How to Use Them

Larimar is traditionally associated with the throat chakra, calm communication, emotional release, and oceanic stillness. Practitioners use it with affirmations that draw on these themes — statements of peace, honest expression, and emotional self-acceptance. This page gives twelve affirmations suited to Larimar's traditional associations and a simple practice method for using them.

## Why Practitioners Pair Affirmations with Larimar

In crystal practice, affirmations are spoken or internally stated intentions that are used to direct focus and reinforce a mental or emotional orientation. Pairing an affirmation with a physical object — a stone held in the hand or placed nearby — is a traditional method used to anchor the intention and make the practice more embodied. This is a matter of personal tradition; the physical stone does not alter the affirmation's words, but practitioners report that the tactile and sensory element of holding a stone helps settle the mind for the practice.

Larimar's traditional associations — throat chakra, emotional calm, clear expression, release of tension — make it most naturally suited to affirmations about communication, emotional well-being, and peace. Affirmations about willpower, action, or physical energy are less typical fits; stones like citrine or carnelian are more often used for those intentions.

For a full account of Larimar's traditional associations, see [Larimar meaning](/gemstones/larimar/meaning). For the site-wide guide on working with crystals intentionally, see [how to program crystals](/learn/how-to-program-crystals).

## Twelve Affirmations for Larimar

Affirmations below are grouped by theme. Use one per session or select whichever feels most relevant to your current focus.

**Calm and emotional peace**
1. I am at peace with what I cannot change.
2. I release what I have been carrying and return to stillness.
3. My mind is calm and my body is at ease.
4. I breathe in calm and breathe out what I no longer need.

**Communication and self-expression**
5. I express myself honestly and with care.
6. I speak my truth with clarity and kindness.
7. My words reflect my real feelings.
8. I listen as openly as I speak.

**Emotional healing and self-compassion**
9. I allow myself to feel and to heal.
10. I am compassionate with myself and patient with my own growth.
11. I release old emotional patterns that no longer serve me.
12. I am open to joy and willing to let go of what has been heavy.

## How to Use Affirmations with Larimar

A simple practice method follows. Adapt it to whatever length and format suits you.

**Step 1: Handle the stone before starting.** Hold the Larimar in your hands for one to two minutes with your eyes closed. Notice the weight and temperature of the stone. This brief period is used in practice to settle the mind before the affirmation proper.

**Step 2: Choose one affirmation.** Select one statement from the list above, or write your own in the same voice and register. Shorter is usually easier to hold in mind through repetition.

**Step 3: Repeat slowly.** Say or think the affirmation three to five times, with a pause between each repetition. Practitioners commonly time each repetition with a breath cycle — inhale, then say the affirmation on the exhale.

**Step 4: Sit quietly for a moment.** After the repetitions, hold the stone and sit quietly for another minute or two without forcing anything further. Some practitioners place the stone on their chest, throat, or in their lap at this stage.

**Step 5: Place or wear the stone.** After the practice, some practitioners keep the stone nearby — in a pocket, on a desk, or at the bedside — as a physical reminder of the intention set during the session.

## Adapting the Practice

**Bedtime use.** Practitioners who use Larimar for sleep-related calm often run a short affirmation practice before bed, then place the stone on the nightstand or in a pouch under the pillow. Affirmations focused on release ("I let go of today's tension") or peace ("I am safe and at rest") fit this context well.

**With combinations.** Larimar is sometimes held alongside a second stone when the intention spans both Larimar's associations and those of a partner stone. For example, holding Larimar and [rose quartz](/gemstones/rose-quartz) together for a self-compassion affirmation is a practice some crystal users describe. See [Larimar combinations](/gemstones/larimar/combinations) for which stones practitioners pair with it.

**Writing instead of speaking.** Some practitioners write the affirmation in a journal with the stone placed beside or on the open page. The spoken and written forms carry no difference in the practice tradition; use whichever is more accessible.

## Physical Care During Practice

Larimar at Mohs 4.5–5 is moderately soft. Hold it gently during the practice and set it down on a soft surface (a cloth, a wooden dish) rather than hard surfaces that risk chipping an edge. Avoid holding Larimar alongside harder stones — [amethyst](/gemstones/amethyst) or [clear quartz](/gemstones/clear-quartz), for example — in the same hand where the stones can rub against each other.
