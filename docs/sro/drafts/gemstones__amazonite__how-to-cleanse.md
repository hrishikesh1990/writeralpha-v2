# How to Cleanse Amazonite Without Damaging It

Amazonite can tolerate a brief rinse under lukewarm water for physical dirt removal, but should never be soaked or used to make a water elixir — the stone contains trace lead that can leach into water under acidic conditions. For crystal-tradition cleansing, smoke, sound, moonlight, and dry earth are all fully safe options. Avoid steam cleaners and ultrasonic cleaners.

## What to Know About Amazonite and Water

Amazonite is a variety of microcline feldspar (KAlSi3O8) with a Mohs hardness of 6 to 6.5. Physically, brief contact with plain lukewarm water — such as rinsing under a tap — will not damage the stone. Jewelers' guidance for cleaning amazonite jewelry is warm soapy water and a soft brush, rinse briefly, and dry immediately.

The significant concern with water is not structural damage to the stone but lead migration. Amazonite's blue-green color is caused in part by trace lead content in its crystal lattice. Laboratory testing has shown that when amazonite is stored in acidic solution — conditions that simulate a stone sitting in water over time — lead leaches out at levels well above the EU toy safety limit. The concern applies specifically to:

- **Water elixirs**: placing amazonite directly in drinking water for hours or days
- **Prolonged soaking**: leaving the stone submerged during cleansing
- **Salt-water baths**: saltwater is mildly acidic and more corrosive than plain water

For a full discussion of amazonite's water safety, see [can amazonite go in water](/gemstones/amazonite/can-go-in-water). For general guidance on water-safe crystals, see [how to cleanse crystals](/learn/how-to-cleanse-crystals).

## Safe Cleansing Methods

### Smoke Cleansing

Hold the amazonite piece in the smoke of burning herbs, incense, or a candle for 30 to 60 seconds. Move the piece slowly so all surfaces pass through the smoke. Smoke cleansing is the most commonly recommended method for amazonite in crystal practice and carries no physical risk to the stone. Ensure the space is ventilated.

### Sound

Place amazonite near a singing bowl, bell, or tuning fork and allow the vibration to move through it for one to two minutes. The stone does not need to be touched or moved into anything liquid. This is particularly convenient for large specimens, pieces in settings, or multiple stones cleansed at once.

### Moonlight

Set amazonite on a dry surface — a windowsill or outdoor table on a clear night — and leave it under moonlight for several hours or overnight. Check the weather forecast first: rain, dew, or high overnight humidity will expose the stone to moisture. Bring it indoors before morning condensation forms. This method is popular in crystal tradition for a combined cleanse and recharge in a single step.

### Dry Earth Burial

Bury amazonite in a small pot of dry indoor potting soil or sand for 24 hours. Dig a shallow hole, place the stone, cover it, and retrieve it the next day. Brush off any soil with a dry cloth or soft brush. Use indoor soil rather than garden soil, which can be damp. This is one of the older grounding practices in crystal tradition.

### Breath and Visualization

Hold the amazonite in both hands, breathe in slowly, and exhale across the surface while focusing on releasing any accumulated energy. Repeat two or three times. This method requires no materials, leaves no residue, and is available anywhere.

### What to Avoid

| Method | Problem |
|---|---|
| Prolonged water soak | Lead leaching risk (ingestion concern) |
| Salt-water bath | Acidic, increases lead migration |
| Steam cleaner | Heat and pressure can damage the stone |
| Ultrasonic cleaner | Vibration can propagate along cleavage planes and split the stone |
| Direct sunlight (long exposure) | Some feldspar specimens may fade slightly with extended UV exposure |

## Recharging After Cleansing

Cleansing and recharging are treated as separate steps in most crystal traditions. After cleansing, place amazonite in morning sunlight for 15 to 30 minutes (brief exposure poses no physical risk), near a cluster of clear quartz, or on a dry selenite slab overnight. Amazonite is traditionally associated with communication and calm — see [crystals for anxiety](/crystals/crystals-for-anxiety) and [crystals for connection](/crystals/crystals-for-connection) for how it fits into broader practice. ## How Often to Cleanse

Crystal traditions vary widely. A common guideline is to cleanse amazonite when it first arrives, after any extended period of daily wear or use, and monthly during regular display. There is no physical basis for any specific schedule — this is a matter of personal practice. As a physical maintenance note, wipe your amazonite periodically with a dry soft cloth to remove surface oils from handling, and store it away from harder stones (quartz, sapphire) that can scratch its surface.
