# Who Should Be Cautious About Wearing Peach Moonstone

Peach Moonstone is not physically toxic and poses no health hazard from normal wear. The real cautions are physical: its Mohs hardness of 6–6.5 and two planes of perfect cleavage make it fragile enough to crack or chip in everyday rings. Astrologically and in crystal tradition, some practitioners advise caution for people with specific birth-chart placements or sensitivity patterns — but those are matters of tradition, not physical risk.

Crystals are a complementary practice. They are not a substitute for medical care.

## Physical Cautions: Who Should Rethink How They Wear It

Peach Moonstone is a variety of orthoclase feldspar with Mohs hardness 6–6.5. This means a steel file, quartz sand, or many ceramic surfaces can scratch it. More importantly, it has two directions of perfect cleavage at approximately right angles — a sharp impact on a hard surface can split the stone cleanly in two, regardless of the force involved.

People who work with their hands — builders, mechanics, gardeners, kitchen workers — should avoid wearing peach moonstone in a ring during those activities. A bump against a granite countertop is sufficient to cleave a cabochon. Pendants and earrings carry much lower risk because they rarely meet hard surfaces.

Children and infants should not handle loose tumbled stones unsupervised due to choking hazard from small pieces; this is a general precaution for all small gemstones.

No dust or contact toxicity hazard has been identified for peach moonstone in normal gemstone use. Cutting or grinding feldspar in industrial settings creates silica dust — a general respiratory hazard — but this does not apply to polished specimens handled as jewelry or display pieces.

## Traditional Cautions in Crystal Practice

Crystal practitioners traditionally describe [peach moonstone](/gemstones/peach-moonstone) as a stone linked to lunar and feminine energy, associated with emotional sensitivity and intuition. Practitioners sometimes suggest that people who are already in a period of intense emotional upheaval take an introductory approach — keeping the stone nearby rather than wearing it continuously — to observe how they respond. This is tradition, not a measurable effect.

Some practitioners also suggest removing peach moonstone during arguments or highly charged situations, based on the belief that it amplifies emotional states. For a broader discussion of what traditions say this stone is for, see [peach moonstone meaning](/gemstones/peach-moonstone/meaning).

For guidance on night-time use, including whether to sleep with peach moonstone under the pillow, see [sleeping with peach moonstone](/gemstones/peach-moonstone/sleeping-with).

## Astrological Cautions (Vedic Tradition)

In Vedic astrology tradition, moonstone is associated with the planet Moon (Chandra) and is primarily recommended for Cancer (Karka Rashi) ascendants and moon signs. According to Vedic practitioners, caution is advised for:

- People whose birth chart shows an already strongly placed or afflicted Moon, where adding a Moon-amplifying stone may intensify existing emotional patterns
- Aries, Leo, Scorpio, and Aquarius ascendants, where the Moon's placement may conflict with the ruling planet — practitioners typically recommend consultation with an astrologer before wearing moonstone

These are matters of traditional practice within Vedic astrology, not scientific determinations. Western astrology does not use the same framework of planetary gem prescriptions.
