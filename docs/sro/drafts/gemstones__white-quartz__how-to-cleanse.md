# How to Cleanse and Recharge White Quartz

White Quartz (SiO2, Mohs 7) is one of the most forgiving stones to cleanse: it is safe in water, tolerates brief sunlight, and accepts salt without damage. Any of the common cleansing methods work safely. The standard approach is a short rinse under running water followed by air drying, or moonlight overnight for an energetic reset.

## What Makes White Quartz Easy to Cleanse

White Quartz is silicon dioxide — chemically inert at normal temperatures, with no soluble compounds, no UV-sensitive color centers (its white opacity comes from light-scattering fluid inclusions, not dye), and a hardness of 7 on the Mohs scale. It does not dissolve in water, does not fade in sunlight, and does not react with salt. This gives you more cleansing options than you would have with fragile or reactive stones.

For context on which stones are water-safe versus not, see [how to cleanse crystals](/learn/how-to-cleanse-crystals). White Quartz's specific water safety is explained in detail at [can White Quartz go in water](/gemstones/white-quartz/can-go-in-water).

## Safe Cleansing Methods

### Running water

Hold White Quartz under cool or lukewarm running tap water for 30 to 60 seconds. Cup your hands so the water washes over all surfaces. Pat dry with a clean cloth immediately and allow to air dry fully before storage. This is the most direct method and works for polished stones, tumbled pieces, and most clusters. Avoid hot water — rapid temperature changes can cause microfractures in any crystal.

### Moonlight

Place White Quartz on a dry windowsill or outside on a clear night and leave it for several hours or overnight. Moonlight is a widely favored method in crystal tradition because it works for virtually every stone and combines cleansing and recharging in one step. Check the weather: if rain or heavy dew is expected, use an indoor windowsill. Bring the stone inside before morning to avoid prolonged direct sunlight if you prefer a gentler treatment.

### Smoke cleansing

Pass White Quartz slowly through the smoke of burning herbs, incense, or a candle for 30 to 60 seconds, rotating the piece so all faces are exposed. This leaves no moisture and is well suited to clusters or large raw pieces that are harder to rinse. Ensure the space is ventilated.

### Sound

Place White Quartz near a singing bowl, bell, or tuning fork and allow the vibration to pass through it for one to two minutes. Sound cleansing works at a distance and requires no direct contact, making it ideal for delicate or heavily included specimens.

### Dry salt or selenite slab

For energetic cleansing without physical contact, place White Quartz on a dry selenite slab or in a bowl of dry sea salt for several hours or overnight. White Quartz tolerates dry salt because it is non-porous and non-reactive. Brush off any loose salt grains with a dry cloth before storage.

### Sunlight

White Quartz is safe in sunlight. Its white or near-colorless appearance has no UV-sensitive color centers, unlike amethyst, rose quartz, or citrine, which fade with prolonged sun exposure. A period of 30 to 60 minutes in morning sunlight both cleanses and recharges the stone in one step according to tradition. Prolonged midday exposure is not necessary and adds unnecessary surface heat.

## Recharging After Cleansing

Crystal tradition treats cleansing and recharging as separate steps. After cleansing, White Quartz is commonly recharged by:

- Placing it in brief morning sunlight for 15 to 30 minutes
- Leaving it on a dry selenite charging slab overnight
- Setting it near or on a cluster of clear quartz
- Leaving it in full moonlight overnight

For a full guide to recharging methods, see [how to charge crystals](/learn/how-to-charge-crystals). White Quartz is also used in crystal tradition as a charging amplifier for other stones, making it one of the few stones that can serve as a charging platform as well as a recipient.

## How Often to Cleanse

Crystal traditions vary on frequency. A widely used guideline: cleanse when the stone arrives, after any intensive use, and monthly for display stones. If you carry White Quartz daily or use it in meditation, once a week is a common recommendation. There is no physical basis for a cleansing schedule — this is a personal practice decision.

As a physical maintenance note, inspect polished White Quartz periodically for surface scratching or dullness, which can appear after years of contact with harder surfaces. The [White Quartz hub page](/gemstones/white-quartz) covers all physical properties in one place.
