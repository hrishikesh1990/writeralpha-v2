# How to Cleanse Lava Stone Without Damaging It

A quick water rinse is safe for lava stone, but prolonged soaking is not recommended because the porous basalt structure holds moisture that degrades elastic cord over time and can encourage mildew in the cavities. The most practical cleansing methods are smoke, sound, moonlight, brief sunlight, and a swift rinse followed by thorough drying. Each is covered below.

## Understanding Lava Stone's Physical Properties

Lava stone is vesicular basalt — a volcanic rock whose characteristic pores were left by gas bubbles as molten lava cooled. Those pores are the same feature that makes lava stone useful as an essential oil diffuser. They also mean that any liquid that enters the stone takes time to leave, particularly on thicker beads or tumbled pieces. Basalt does not react chemically with water the way iron-bearing minerals like pyrite do, so a brief rinse carries no corrosion risk. The practical concern is mechanical: water trapped in pores puts stress on elastic cord in bracelets and creates conditions for mold if the stone is stored damp.

Salt water and dry salt burial carry the same caveat. Salt is hygroscopic — it draws ambient moisture — and salt residue left in pores can continue attracting dampness after the stone appears dry. If you use a salt rinse, follow it with a plain water rinse and dry the stone completely before storage.

## Safe Cleansing Methods

### Smoke cleansing

Hold lava stone in the smoke of burning herbs, incense, or a candle for 30 to 60 seconds, moving the piece slowly so all surfaces are exposed. This is the most widely used cleansing method for porous stones because it reaches the surface without introducing moisture. Ensure the room is ventilated. Any incense or herb that produces clean smoke without resin buildup on the stone works well.

### Sound

Place lava stone near a singing bowl, bell, or tuning fork and allow the vibration to pass through it for one to two minutes. The stone does not need to be touched or moved. Sound cleansing is particularly practical for bracelets, where soaking poses the greatest cord-degradation risk.

### Moonlight

Set lava stone on a dry surface — a windowsill or outdoor table on a clear night — and leave it under moonlight for several hours or overnight. Check the forecast first: dew, rain, or high overnight humidity will introduce the prolonged moisture the stone should avoid. Bring it indoors before morning condensation forms. This is one of the most popular methods in crystal tradition and leaves no physical residue.

### Brief sunlight

Lava stone is an opaque mineral and does not fade in sunlight. Placing it in morning sun for 15 to 30 minutes is safe and commonly used as both a cleansing and recharging step. Avoid extended midday sun on bracelets with elastic cord, as UV exposure degrades elastomer over time.

### Quick water rinse

Hold lava stone under cool running water for 20 to 30 seconds. Pat dry immediately with a cloth, then set the stone on a dry surface in a warm, ventilated spot for at least an hour before storage. For bracelets, remove the cord if possible while drying; if not, allow extra time. Do not soak. This method is suited for a quick reset between uses rather than a deep cleanse.

## Recharging After Cleansing

In crystal tradition, cleansing and recharging are separate steps. After cleansing, place lava stone in morning sunlight for 15 to 30 minutes, on a dry selenite slab, or near a clear quartz cluster. Lava stone is traditionally associated with grounding and strength — practitioners treat it as a stone that benefits from reconnection with elemental energy (earth and fire associations are common in crystal practice). For a full guide to recharging methods, see [how to charge crystals](/learn/how-to-charge-crystals).

## How Often to Cleanse

Crystal tradition offers no single standard. A common guideline is to cleanse lava stone when it first arrives, after any extended period of wear or use, and monthly during regular display. If you use lava stone as an essential oil diffuser bracelet, wipe the beads with a dry cloth between oil applications to prevent oil buildup in the pores, which can become rancid over time. There is no physical basis for a cleansing schedule; this is a matter of personal practice.

## Storage After Cleansing

Store lava stone in a dry location away from bathrooms or steam-prone areas. A cloth pouch or an open display in a dry room is suitable.
