# How to Cleanse Smoky Quartz Without Damaging It

Smoky Quartz can be rinsed briefly in cool water — it is not water-soluble — but prolonged sunlight should be avoided because the color centers that create the smoky hue are sensitive to UV and will fade. Safe cleansing methods include running water, smoke, sound, moonlight, and earth burial. Each is covered below.

## What Can Damage Smoky Quartz During Cleansing

Smoky Quartz is silicon dioxide (SiO2) with a Mohs hardness of 7. It is harder than glass and durable under normal handling. Two specific conditions can alter it physically or aesthetically.

**Prolonged heat or UV exposure fades the color.** The smoky color comes from aluminum-related color centers formed by natural irradiation in the host rock. These centers are temperature-sensitive: Mindat documents that color fades at temperatures above 200–300°C and that prolonged exposure to UV light — including direct sunlight over days or weeks — can gradually bleach the stone. A short cleanse in indirect light poses no risk, but leaving smoky quartz on a sunny windowsill long-term is not recommended.

**Salt contact.** Dry or wet salt can scratch polished surfaces over time and, if salt residue remains wet against the stone, can etch softer inclusions or the surface of settings in jewelry. A brief rinse to remove salt is fine; extended salt baths are unnecessary.

## Safe Cleansing Methods

### Running water

Hold the smoky quartz under cool or lukewarm running tap water for 30 to 60 seconds. Move the stone so all faces are exposed. Pat dry with a soft cloth immediately after and keep it away from direct sunlight while it dries. This is the simplest method for tumbled stones and most finished jewelry. Do not use hot water, which could stress the stone through rapid temperature change.

### Smoke cleansing

Pass the stone slowly through the smoke of burning herbs, incense, or a candle for 30 to 60 seconds. Ensure the stone does not come into direct contact with the flame. Ventilate the room during and after. Smoke cleansing carries no physical risk to smoky quartz and is a widely used method in crystal practice for clearing accumulated energy.

### Sound

Place smoky quartz near a singing bowl, bell, or tuning fork and allow the sound vibration to pass through it for one to two minutes. The stone does not need to be touched or moved. Sound cleansing is useful for large clusters or raw specimens that are harder to move safely through smoke or water.

### Moonlight

Set the stone on a dry surface — a windowsill, a table outdoors — on a clear night and leave it under moonlight for several hours or overnight. Check the forecast: dew, rain, or high overnight humidity will wet the stone, which is not damaging but adds an unnecessary drying step. Bring the stone indoors before morning sun hits it. Moonlight cleansing is traditional for a gentle overnight reset.

### Dry earth burial

Bury smoky quartz in a small pot of dry indoor potting soil or sand for 24 hours. Dig a shallow hole, place the stone, cover it, and retrieve it the next day. Brush off loose material with a dry cloth. Use indoor or bagged soil rather than garden soil, which may be damp. This method grounds the stone physically and is used in crystal tradition as a deep cleanse.

### Selenite or clear quartz proximity

Placing smoky quartz on a selenite slab or beside a clear quartz cluster for several hours is used in crystal practice as a passive cleanse. There is no physical mechanism for this, but the method carries no risk of physical damage. Note that selenite itself dissolves in prolonged water contact, so keep the two stones dry.

## How Often to Cleanse

Crystal traditions vary. A common guideline is to cleanse smoky quartz when it first arrives, after any period of intensive use, and once a month during regular display. There is no physical basis for a specific cleansing schedule — this is a matter of personal practice. As a general maintenance point, inspect the stone periodically for surface scratches or residue and clean gently with a damp cloth as needed.

## Recharging After Cleansing

Cleansing and recharging are treated as separate steps in crystal tradition. After cleansing, common recharging methods for smoky quartz include brief morning sunlight (15 to 30 minutes — short exposure does not fade the stone), placement near a clear quartz cluster, or resting on a dry selenite slab.
