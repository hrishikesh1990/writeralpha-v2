# Black Onyx Affirmations — Phrases and How to Use Them

Affirmations suited to black onyx draw on its traditional associations: protection, grounding, inner strength, and steadiness in adversity. The practice involves holding or touching the stone while speaking or silently repeating a phrase — the stone serves as a physical anchor for the intention. Below are twelve affirmations organized by the theme they address, followed by practical guidance on timing and use.

## What the Affirmations Are Based On

Black onyx is traditionally associated in crystal practice with protection (as a shield from external disturbances), grounding (connection to the present moment and the body), inner strength (endurance under pressure), and confidence. These associations are tradition, not verified outcomes. Affirmations work as a self-directed attention and intention practice; the stone's role is as a tactile focal point. Neither affirmations nor crystals are treatments for anxiety, depression, or any medical condition.

For the full traditional profile and history of black onyx's associations, see [black onyx meaning](/gemstones/black-onyx/meaning) and the stone's hub page at [black onyx](/gemstones/black-onyx).

## Affirmations by Theme

### Protection

1. "I am surrounded by a boundary of calm and safety."
2. "I release what is not mine to carry."
3. "I am grounded, centered, and protected in this moment."
4. "Disturbance moves around me; I remain steady."

### Grounding and presence

5. "I am here. I am solid. I am enough."
6. "My roots go deep and hold me steady."
7. "I bring my attention fully to where I am right now."
8. "I am connected to the ground beneath me and to my own strength."

### Inner strength and resilience

9. "I have the endurance to meet what is in front of me."
10. "Difficulty does not diminish me; it shows me what I am made of."
11. "I choose steadiness over reactivity."

### Confidence

12. "I trust my own judgment and act from my own center."

## How to Use Affirmations with Black Onyx

### Step 1: Prepare the stone

Cleanse the stone before establishing a regular affirmation practice — see [how to cleanse black onyx](/gemstones/black-onyx/how-to-cleanse) for options. This is a traditional step; physically, the stone is unchanged by cleansing.

### Step 2: Choose one or two affirmations

More than two or three affirmations in a single session tends to dilute focus. Choose the phrase that most directly addresses your current intention and stay with it.

### Step 3: Hold the stone and settle your attention

Sit comfortably. Hold the black onyx in your dominant or non-dominant hand — whichever feels natural. Take two or three slow breaths to bring attention to the physical sensation of holding the stone: its weight, temperature, texture. This is the grounding step before the affirmation.

### Step 4: Speak or silently repeat the affirmation

Say the chosen affirmation aloud or silently, slowly, three to seven times. Pause between repetitions. The intention is attentive repetition, not mechanical recitation — bring your full attention to the meaning of each word.

### Step 5: Close the practice

Return your attention to the physical sensation of the stone, take one more slow breath, and set it down. The entire practice can take two to five minutes.

## Timing

Practitioners use stone-and-affirmation practice at various points in the day:

- **Morning**: Setting intention for the day ahead; useful for the confidence and strength affirmations
- **Evening or before sleep**: Settling the mind and releasing the day; protective and grounding affirmations suit this timing; see also the [sleeping with black onyx](/gemstones/black-onyx/sleeping-with) page
- **During stress or transition**: A one- or two-minute practice in a difficult moment; the physical act of holding the stone provides a grounding anchor

## Pairing with Other Stones for Affirmations

Black onyx is sometimes paired with other stones in affirmation practice, where one stone anchors grounding and another carries a complementary intention. Clear quartz alongside black onyx is a common combination for clarity-and-strength intentions; smoky quartz is used for deeper grounding practices.
