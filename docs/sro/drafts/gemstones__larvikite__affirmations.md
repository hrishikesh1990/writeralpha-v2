# Larvikite Affirmations — Words That Suit This Stone

Affirmations used with larvikite in crystal practice draw on its traditional associations: grounding, protection, psychic discernment, and inner stability. The affirmations below are matched to those themes and can be used held in the hand, worn, or placed nearby during a seated practice.

## Why Affirmations and Larvikite Are Used Together

In crystal healing tradition, affirmations and crystals are paired on the principle that both work through intention — the stone provides a physical anchor for a chosen mental or emotional focus, and the spoken or internally-held affirmation gives that focus a specific direction. This is a practice of intention and attention; no physical mechanism is proposed.

Larvikite's traditional profile — grounding, protective, associated with the Root chakra, and linked to psychic awareness — makes it particularly suited to affirmations about stability, boundaries, and inner knowing. For the full traditional associations, see [larvikite meaning](/gemstones/larvikite/meaning). For a broader guide to programming crystals with intention, see [how to program crystals](/learn/how-to-program-crystals).

## Affirmations for Grounding and Stability

Grounding affirmations suit practitioners who feel scattered, anxious, or overwhelmed:

- "I am grounded, stable, and fully present in this moment."
- "My feet are planted on the earth. I am safe and supported."
- "I release what is out of my control and return to what is real."
- "I am rooted in my own strength and calm."
- "My energy is centered. I move through the day with steadiness."

## Affirmations for Protection and Boundaries

Protection affirmations suit practitioners who feel energetically drained, overstimulated by others, or seeking to strengthen personal limits:

- "I am protected. What does not serve my highest good cannot reach me."
- "I hold my center. I give what I choose to give, and I keep the rest."
- "My boundaries are clear and they are respected."
- "I move through the world without absorbing what is not mine."
- "I am a clear channel for my own wisdom, unaffected by outside noise."

## Affirmations for Intuition and Inner Knowing

Larvikite is traditionally associated with psychic discernment — the ability to perceive truth through an inner sense. These affirmations match that quality:

- "I trust my inner knowing. My intuition is clear and reliable."
- "I see beyond the surface to what is true."
- "My perception is sharp. I recognize what resonates and what does not."
- "I am open to wisdom that comes from within."
- "I move through the unknown with curiosity and calm."

## Affirmations for Emotional Stability and Healing

For periods of grief, transition, or emotional difficulty:

- "I allow what I feel to pass through me without holding me back."
- "I am strong enough to face what is difficult and wise enough to let it go."
- "Each day I return to myself, grounded and whole."
- "I give myself permission to feel and then to move forward."
- "My roots are deep. I can weather this."

## How to Use Affirmations with Larvikite: Step by Step

1. **Choose your stone.** A tumbled piece, palm stone, or sphere is easy to hold. If wearing larvikite as a bracelet or pendant, you can use it in place or hold the jewelry in your hands.

2. **Find a quiet moment.** Two to five minutes is enough. Sitting is more effective than standing for this practice because it reinforces the grounding quality of larvikite.

3. **Hold the stone in both hands.** Feel its weight and temperature. The stone's dark surface and slight coolness in the hand are often noted as part of the grounding quality in this practice.

4. **Take three slow breaths.** Each exhale should be longer than the inhale. This is a standard calming technique that pairs naturally with a grounding stone practice.

5. **Speak or internally repeat your chosen affirmation.** Say it clearly, three to seven times. Spoken affirmations are traditional; silent repetition works equally well.

6. **Rest in the feeling the affirmation points toward.** Do not rush to the next thing. Allow 30 to 60 seconds of quiet after repeating the affirmation.

7. **Close the practice.** Set the stone down intentionally, or wear it with awareness of the intention you have set. Some practitioners express brief gratitude for the practice; this is personal preference.
