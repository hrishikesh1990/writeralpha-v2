# Sleeping with Citrine — What to Expect

Citrine is a variety of quartz (SiO₂, Mohs 7) with no toxicity, making it physically safe to keep in a bedroom or near a sleeping area. In crystal tradition, citrine is associated with joy, confidence, and abundance — qualities practitioners often want to carry into sleep and morning. Some practitioners report that citrine's traditionally energizing associations make it too stimulating for sleep for them personally; others keep it by the bed without issue.

Crystals are a complementary practice, not a substitute for medical care or professional advice on sleep concerns.

## What Crystal Tradition Says About Citrine at Night

Citrine is traditionally associated with solar energy, the fire element, and the solar plexus chakra — associations that emphasize vitality and confidence rather than the cooling, calming qualities more often linked to sleep stones. In contrast to stones like [amethyst](/gemstones/amethyst) or [moonstone](/gemstones/moonstone), which are typically recommended in the [crystals for sleep](/crystals/crystals-for-sleep) guide, citrine is less commonly placed in that category.

That said, practitioners who work with citrine for abundance or confidence intentions — particularly those who want to set morning intentions — sometimes keep it at the bedside rather than under the pillow, so its presence is felt on waking rather than throughout the night.

In Vedic tradition, citrine is linked to the Sun and is sometimes recommended for those seeking to boost willpower and clarity. The same solar associations explain why some practitioners prefer to use it in daytime rather than sleep settings.

## Who May Find It Too Stimulating

Crystal practice literature suggests that people who are already prone to racing thoughts, high mental energy at night, or vivid dreaming may find citrine less restful as a sleep companion. This is framed as individual sensitivity to the stone's traditional energy rather than a physical effect.

If you fall into this group, keeping citrine on a bedside table — nearby but not directly under the pillow — is a middle ground some practitioners use. Alternatively, the stone can be placed on the windowsill or a shelf to maintain proximity without direct contact during sleep.

For a broader look at who may wish to exercise caution with citrine, see [who should not wear citrine](/gemstones/citrine/who-should-not-wear).

## How Practitioners Use Citrine at Night

The most common approaches described in crystal practice:

1. **Under the pillow.** Place a small, smooth tumbled citrine (not a raw cluster with sharp points) under or inside the pillowcase. Tumbled stones are safer physically and less likely to shift during sleep. Intention is typically set before sleeping — a brief statement of what you want to carry into the next day.

2. **On the bedside table.** Place citrine on the surface nearest to where you sleep. This is the gentler option and is the one more commonly recommended for those who find under-pillow placement disruptive to sleep.

3. **At the foot of the bed.** Some practitioners place citrine at the foot of the bed to work with its associations without it being directly near the head. This approach appears in Feng Shui-influenced crystal placement guides.

4. **Morning routine anchor.** Rather than sleeping with citrine, some practitioners pick it up first thing in the morning and hold it while setting daily intentions. This keeps the stone's energizing associations in a context that suits them — see [citrine affirmations](/gemstones/citrine/affirmations) for intention-setting prompts.

## Physical Safety Considerations

Citrine at Mohs 7 is durable enough for bedside and occasional physical contact. A few practical points:

- **Raw clusters and points** have sharp terminations that can scratch skin or scratch other surfaces; tumbled stones are safer for under-pillow use.
- **Citrine is not toxic.** Quartz is chemically stable and poses no toxicity risk through normal handling or skin contact.
- **Children and pets.** Small tumbled stones are a choking hazard for young children and pets; keep them on elevated surfaces if relevant to your household.

## Setting Intentions Before Sleep

If you use citrine at night, practitioners typically recommend a brief intention-setting moment before sleeping: hold the stone, state what you are working toward (abundance, confidence, creative clarity), and place it in your chosen position.
