# Obsidian Affirmations — How to Use Them With the Stone

Obsidian affirmations draw on the stone's traditional associations with protection, grounding, truth, and transformation. The practice is to hold the stone while repeating the affirmation aloud or silently, using the physical weight and coolness of the volcanic glass as an anchor for the spoken intention. No specific outcome is promised; this is a traditional mindfulness and intention-setting practice.

## Why Affirmations Are Used With Obsidian

In crystal tradition, obsidian is described as a stone of inner reflection — it is associated with honest self-examination, releasing what no longer serves, and maintaining protective boundaries. Affirmations paired with obsidian are therefore framed around truth-telling, strength, and grounding, rather than around attracting or acquiring. The stone's weight and opacity are used as a physical cue: heavy, dark, solid, rooted.

For obsidian's full traditional meaning and associations, see [obsidian meaning](/gemstones/obsidian/meaning). For the general practice of setting intentions with crystals, see [how to program crystals](/learn/how-to-program-crystals).

## How to Use an Affirmation With Obsidian

1. Choose one affirmation that matches your current intention. Using one at a time is more effective than cycling through several.
2. Hold the obsidian stone in both hands, or in the dominant hand. A tumbled stone, palmstone, or sphere all work well for this. Ensure the piece is polished if you are holding it for extended periods — raw obsidian has sharp edges.
3. Take three slow breaths to settle and arrive in the present.
4. Repeat the affirmation aloud three times, or silently if privacy is needed. Speak at normal pace; there is no benefit to rushing.
5. Sit with the stone and the statement for a moment, noticing any physical sensation of weight in the hands or the breath settling.
6. Place the stone somewhere visible — on a desk, beside a door, on a windowsill — as a reminder during the day.

This process takes approximately two to three minutes. Longer is not necessarily better; consistency across days matters more than duration in any single session.

## Affirmations for Protection

Protection affirmations draw on obsidian's primary traditional association — creating an energetic boundary and deflecting unwanted energy. See [crystals for protection](/crystals/crystals-for-protection) for how obsidian fits alongside other protective stones.

- "I am protected and safe in this moment."
- "I release what does not belong to me and hold only what is mine."
- "My boundaries are clear, firm, and respected."
- "I move through the world protected and grounded."
- "Nothing that is not for my highest good can reach me here."

## Affirmations for Grounding

Obsidian's volcanic earth origin is used in grounding practice to anchor scattered or anxious energy. See [crystals for grounding](/crystals/crystals-for-grounding) for companion stones.

- "I am rooted, present, and grounded in this moment."
- "I release what I cannot control and return to what is solid and real."
- "My feet are on the ground. I am here."
- "I trust the process of change and remain steady through it."
- "I am connected to the earth and fully present in my body."

## Affirmations for Truth and Shadow Work

Truth and shadow work affirmations align with obsidian's association with honest self-examination and releasing patterns that are no longer useful.

- "I see myself clearly and accept what I find with honesty and compassion."
- "I release the patterns that no longer serve me."
- "I am willing to face what is true, even when it is uncomfortable."
- "I transform what I find in the dark into understanding and strength."
- "The truth about who I am is something I can meet with courage."

## Affirmations for Transformation

For people using obsidian during significant life transitions.

- "I am in transformation and I trust the direction."
- "I let go of the old version of myself with gratitude."
- "Change moves through me and leaves me stronger."
- "I am not afraid of becoming who I am meant to be."

## Bedtime Use

For using obsidian affirmations as part of a sleep routine, choose one of the protection or grounding phrases above. Hold the stone, repeat the affirmation three times, then place the stone on the bedside table or under the bed. Pairing the affirmation with a physical placement ritual creates a consistent cue that is easy to sustain nightly.
