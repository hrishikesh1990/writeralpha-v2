# How to Cleanse Black Tourmaline Without Damaging It

Black tourmaline tolerates a brief rinse under running water, but soaking is not recommended. The stone's characteristic vertical striations contain microscopic fissures; prolonged water exposure can seep into these and eventually cause flaking or structural damage. Iron in the stone's chemistry can also interact with moisture over time. Safe cleansing methods include smoke, sound, moonlight, a selenite plate, and dry earth. Each is covered below.

## Water and Black Tourmaline: The Short Version

Black tourmaline (schorl) sits at Mohs 7 and is not water-soluble, so brief contact with water does not dissolve or chemically corrode the stone the way it does minerals like selenite or calcite. A 30-second rinse under cool running water to remove dust or surface grime is physically safe.

What to avoid is soaking. Black tourmaline's vertical striations — the defining grooves that run along its crystal length — contain natural microscopic fissures. Water can enter these fissures during extended immersion, and over repeated soakings or long baths, internal damage can develop. Salt water is worse: salt is abrasive, can lodge in the striations, and draws ambient moisture toward the surface.

## Safe Cleansing Methods

### Smoke cleansing

Hold black tourmaline in the smoke of burning herbs, incense, or a candle for 30 to 60 seconds, moving it slowly so all surfaces pass through the smoke. This is the most widely used cleansing method for this stone in crystal practice and carries no physical risk. Ensure the room is ventilated; you are breathing the smoke as well as the stone.

This is a traditional practice from various Indigenous and folk traditions. Choose herbs that feel meaningful to your practice; there is no physical requirement.

### Sound

Place black tourmaline near a singing bowl, bell, or tuning fork and allow the vibration to pass through it for one to two minutes. The stone never needs to be moved or touched by anything during sound cleansing, making it safe for raw specimens, rough chunks, or large pieces that would be awkward to smudge. Sound cleansing works at a short distance; the bowl does not need to be in direct contact with the stone.

### Moonlight

Set black tourmaline on a dry windowsill or outdoor surface on a clear night and leave it under moonlight for several hours or overnight. Check the weather before setting it outside: dew, rain, or high overnight humidity will expose the stone to moisture you want to avoid. Bring it back indoors before morning condensation forms. Full moon placement is most common in crystal tradition, where it is considered a combined cleansing and recharging step.

### Selenite plate or slab

Place black tourmaline on or beside a dry selenite slab for several hours. Selenite is used in crystal tradition as a self-cleansing mineral that neutralizes absorbed energy in nearby stones. This is a no-contact, no-moisture method. Note: do not store the two stones together in a bag or box where they will rub against each other — [selenite](/gemstones/selenite) is very soft at Mohs 2 and will be scratched by black tourmaline at Mohs 7.

### Dry earth burial

Bury black tourmaline in a small pot of dry indoor potting soil or sand for 24 hours. Dig a shallow hole, place the stone, cover it lightly, and retrieve it the next day. Brush off loose soil with a dry, soft-bristled brush. Use indoor potting soil rather than garden soil, which may be damp. This method is used in crystal tradition to connect the stone to the earth and release accumulated energy.

### Breath and visualization

Hold the stone in both hands, take a slow breath in, and exhale steadily across the surface while focusing on releasing any accumulated energy. Repeat two or three times. This requires no materials and leaves no physical residue. It is commonly used between other cleansing sessions or when other methods are not available.

## Recharging After Cleansing

Crystal tradition treats cleansing and recharging as separate steps. After cleansing, common recharging methods for black tourmaline include:

- **Brief morning sunlight.** 15 to 30 minutes of direct sun poses no fading or bleaching risk to an opaque, iron-rich mineral. Avoid extended hours in a hot car or midday sun, which can cause thermal shock in any stone with internal fissures.
- **A selenite slab.** Placing the cleansed stone on selenite overnight is a method used both to cleanse and to recharge in a single step.
- **Clear quartz cluster.** Set black tourmaline near or on a [clear quartz](/gemstones/clear-quartz) cluster and leave it for several hours.
- **Intention and sound.** State your intention for the stone aloud or in writing while holding it; use a singing bowl or tuning fork alongside for a combined recharge.

For a full guide to recharging approaches, see [how to charge crystals](/learn/how-to-charge-crystals).

## How Often to Cleanse

Crystal tradition varies on schedule. Common guidance is to cleanse black tourmaline when it first arrives, after any period of intense use, and monthly during regular display. Daily-wear jewelry or stones kept in a [protective](/crystals/crystals-for-protection) or [grounding](/crystals/crystals-for-grounding) purpose at the front door or on a desk are often cleansed more frequently — some practitioners do this weekly.

There is no physical basis for a cleansing schedule; this is a matter of personal practice. As a physical maintenance note, inspect your black tourmaline periodically for any unusual surface changes, and keep it away from prolonged moisture, strong chemical cleaners, or ultrasonic cleaning devices, which can exploit existing fissures.
