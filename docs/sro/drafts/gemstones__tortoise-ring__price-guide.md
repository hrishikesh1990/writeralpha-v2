# Tortoise Ring Price Guide by Metal and Weight

A sterling silver (925) tortoise ring in India retails for approximately ₹1,200–₹3,700 per ring (roughly $14–$45 USD at September 2026 exchange rates), depending on weight and design complexity. A 22K gold tortoise ring in the same market ranges from ₹48,000 to ₹90,000 or more (roughly $570–$1,070 USD), driven almost entirely by metal weight and the gold spot price. Copper and brass pieces are far cheaper but are rarely sold as talismans by reputable retailers.

All prices below are retail, inclusive of tax where stated, and were checked at named Indian and international retailers in September 2026. Prices vary by seller, gold spot price on the date of purchase, and making charges.

Sterling silver tortoise rings are the most common version of this talisman and the most commercially available. Observed retail prices from Indian online retailers in September 2026:

The per-gram silver price at Indian retailers is approximately ₹230–₹260 per gram of fine silver as of late 2025 to mid-2026 (one retailer listed ₹235/g). Making charges (labour) typically add ₹500–₹1,500 on top of metal value for a designed piece like a tortoise ring.

**What drives the silver price:** Metal weight is the primary factor — a heavier ring with more silver costs more. Design complexity (three-dimensional shell carving, oxidized detail, stone inset) adds making charges. CZ (cubic zirconia) stone accents add minimal cost. A BIS hallmark and authenticity certificate are expected at the higher end of the range.

**International buyers:** A handful of silver tortoise rings appear on international platforms (Etsy, specialist Indian jewellery exporters) at equivalent prices ranging from approximately $15 to $50 USD per piece, including export margins.

At the Khazana price breakdown, gold at ₹15,010/g and 4.885g gross weight gives a metal value of approximately ₹73,300, with ₹14,980 in making charges — showing that making charges on a detailed tortoise design are substantial relative to a plain band.

**What drives the gold price:**
- **Metal weight:** Every gram of 22K gold at September 2026 spot adds roughly ₹14,000–₹15,000 to the base cost (spot prices fluctuate daily).
- **Making charges:** Detailed castings like a tortoise motif incur higher making charges than plain bands, typically ₹2,500–₹5,000/g for handcrafted work.
- **Karat:** An 18K ring uses 75% gold per gram, which reduces metal cost but also reduces the ring's gold content.
- **Stone setting:** A small colored stone or CZ adds a few hundred rupees in most cases.

## Copper, Brass, and Plated Tortoise Ring Prices

Solid copper tortoise rings and copper- or silver-plated brass rings are widely available in Indian street markets and unbranded online listings, typically priced between ₹100 and ₹500 per piece. These are fashion or devotional items and are not hallmarked. Their low price reflects the cost of the base metal (copper costs roughly ₹1/gram at commodity rates) and minimal making charges. No major named jewellery retailer was found listing solid copper tortoise rings in September 2026; the copper category is dominated by unbranded market sellers.

**Buyer's note on plated rings:** A silver-plated ring at ₹200–₹600 is not a discounted 925 ring — it is a brass or zinc core with a micron-thin silver coating. The [how to identify](/gemstones/tortoise-ring/how-to-identify) page explains how to distinguish plated from solid silver using hallmarks and simple tests.

## Jade-Set Tortoise Ring Prices

A tortoise ring set with a carved [jade](/gemstones/jade) tortoise (as opposed to an all-metal sculpted ring) spans a wide range depending on whether the jade is genuine nephrite/jadeite or dyed quartzite, and whether the setting is silver or gold. Genuine jade carvings in a silver setting typically add ₹1,000–₹5,000 to the silver ring base price for a small carved piece. High-grade jadeite is significantly more expensive and is uncommon in mass-market rings.
