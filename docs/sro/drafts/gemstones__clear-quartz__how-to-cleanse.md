# How to Cleanse and Recharge Clear Quartz

Clear quartz (SiO2, Mohs 7) is one of the most physically durable crystals and tolerates water, sunlight, smoke, and sound without damage. You can cleanse it using any of the common methods: running water, saltwater, moonlight, sunlight (brief), smoke, sound, or dry earth burial. Each method and its practical steps are covered below.

## Is Clear Quartz Safe for Water Cleansing?

Yes. Clear quartz is chemically inert and has a Mohs hardness of 7, which means it does not dissolve, soften, or react with water. Brief contact with tap water, spring water, or even saltwater poses no physical risk to polished quartz. For details on all forms of water exposure — extended soaks, salt baths, and sea water — see [can clear quartz go in water](/gemstones/clear-quartz/can-go-in-water).

For a guide to cleansing crystals in general, including which stones are not water-safe, see [how to cleanse crystals](/learn/how-to-cleanse-crystals).

## Cleansing Methods

### Running water

Hold the clear quartz under a stream of cool running water for 30 to 60 seconds. Rotate it so all surfaces are rinsed. Natural running water (a stream or spring) is preferred in crystal tradition, but tap water works equally well from a physical standpoint. Pat the stone dry with a clean cloth and allow it to air dry fully before storing.

### Saltwater

Dissolve one tablespoon of sea salt or Himalayan salt in a cup of water and submerge the quartz for up to a few hours. Rinse thoroughly with fresh water afterward to remove any salt residue from surface features. Avoid prolonged saltwater soaks for pieces with metal wire wrapping, as the metal — not the quartz — may corrode.

### Moonlight

Place clear quartz on a dry windowsill or outdoors on a clean, dry surface on a clear night. Leave it for several hours or overnight, particularly around the full moon, which is traditional for a full reset. Clear quartz does not absorb moisture from mild outdoor conditions the way iron-bearing minerals do, so this is a low-risk method. Bring it indoors before heavy dew or rain.

### Sunlight

Clear quartz is colorless, so it does not fade in sunlight the way colored varieties (amethyst, rose quartz, citrine) do. Short exposure — one to two hours — is physically harmless. Traditional practice treats sunlight as a recharging rather than cleansing method; you can combine both steps by leaving the quartz in morning sunlight. Note that a large, clear quartz sphere or lens-shaped piece can focus sunlight and pose a fire risk; keep it away from flammable materials.

### Smoke cleansing

Hold the quartz in the smoke of burning herbs, incense, or a candle for 30 to 60 seconds, rotating it slowly so all surfaces pass through the smoke. Ensure adequate ventilation. Smoke leaves no physical residue on the stone and is suitable for any quartz piece, including those in delicate settings that cannot be wetted.

### Sound

Place clear quartz near a singing bowl, tuning fork, or bell and allow the vibration to pass through it for one to two minutes. Sound cleansing works at a distance, so the stone does not need to be moved or touched with anything. It is particularly useful for large clusters or sphere stands that are awkward to move.

### Dry earth burial

Place the quartz in a small pot of dry indoor soil or clean sand and leave it buried for 24 hours. Brush off loose material with a dry cloth or soft brush. This grounds the stone physically and is used in tradition for a deep reset. Use indoor potting soil rather than damp garden soil.

### Visualization or breath

Hold the quartz in both hands, inhale slowly, and exhale steadily across the surface while directing your attention toward clearing any accumulated energy. Repeat two or three times. This method requires no materials and is used for a quick reset between uses or when other methods are not available.

## Recharging After Cleansing

In crystal tradition, cleansing and recharging are treated as separate steps. After cleansing, clear quartz is typically recharged by:

- Placing it in direct morning sunlight for 15 to 30 minutes
- Setting it on a dry selenite slab overnight
- Placing it near an amethyst or quartz cluster
- Holding it during meditation while setting a clear intention

For a full guide to recharging methods across different stones, see [how to charge crystals](/learn/how-to-charge-crystals).

## How Often to Cleanse

Crystal tradition offers varying guidance. A widely used approach is to cleanse clear quartz when it arrives, after any extended period of use, and monthly during regular display. Pieces used frequently in hands-on practice are often cleansed more often — weekly or after each session. There is no physical indicator that cleansing is needed; this is a matter of personal practice.

## Storage After Cleansing

Clear quartz does not require special storage conditions. A cool, dry location away from direct long-term sunlight is fine for display. Avoid storing it in contact with soft stones that could be scratched by its Mohs 7 surface (for example, selenite at Mohs 2, or calcite at Mohs 3).
