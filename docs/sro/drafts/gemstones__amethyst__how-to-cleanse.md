# How to Cleanse Amethyst Without Damaging It

Amethyst can be cleansed with warm fresh water, smoke, sound, or moonlight. It is a variety of quartz (SiO2, Mohs 7) and handles brief water exposure well, but its purple color can fade with prolonged exposure to intense sunlight and it should never be exposed to steam or sudden temperature changes. Each method is covered below.

## Physical Cleaning vs. Energetic Cleansing

Two distinct activities go by the name "cleansing" with crystals. Physical cleaning removes dust, skin oils, or residue from the surface. Energetic cleansing is a practice in crystal tradition intended to clear accumulated or stagnant energy from the stone. The methods below address both, and some serve both purposes at once.

For a broader guide to which stones tolerate which methods, see [how to cleanse crystals](/learn/how-to-cleanse-crystals). For the specific question of whether amethyst can go in water, see [amethyst can-go-in-water](/gemstones/amethyst/can-go-in-water).

## Water Cleansing

Amethyst is safe to rinse under cool or lukewarm running fresh water. Hold the piece for 20 to 30 seconds, rubbing gently with your fingers or a soft brush to remove surface dust or oils, then pat dry with a soft cloth.

**Avoid:**
- **Prolonged soaking.** Brief rinsing is fine; leaving amethyst submerged for hours is unnecessary and may affect mounted settings over time.
- **Salt water.** The site's [can-go-in-water](/gemstones/amethyst/can-go-in-water) guidance advises fresh water only. Salt is mildly abrasive and can work into surface cracks in lower-quality or heavily included material.
- **Steam cleaning.** GIA explicitly advises against steam cleaning amethyst, as heat can affect the stone's color centers and the abrupt temperature change risks fracturing.
- **Hot water.** Abrupt temperature changes — including moving from hot to cold water — can cause amethyst to fracture.

## Sunlight Cleansing

Amethyst should not be left in direct sunlight for extended periods. Its purple color is produced by iron impurities (Fe3+) acting on natural radiation-induced color centers in the quartz lattice. Prolonged exposure to intense light — particularly UV — gradually bleaches these centers. GIA notes that some amethyst color fades with prolonged exposure to intense light.

Brief morning sunlight of 15 to 30 minutes is commonly used in crystal practice as a charging step and carries low physical risk. As a general rule, do not leave amethyst on a sunny windowsill for hours or display it in direct afternoon sun over months.

## Smoke Cleansing

Hold the amethyst in the smoke of burning herbs, incense, or a candle for 30 to 60 seconds, turning it so all surfaces pass through the smoke. This is among the most widely used cleansing methods for amethyst in crystal tradition and carries no physical risk to the stone. Ensure the room is ventilated.

## Sound Cleansing

Place amethyst near a singing bowl, bell, or tuning fork and allow the vibration to pass through it for one to two minutes. Sound cleansing works at a distance, which makes it useful for large specimens or clusters that cannot be easily moved through smoke or water. This is a popular method in crystal tradition for clearing energy without any physical contact.

## Moonlight Cleansing

Set amethyst on a dry windowsill or outdoors on a clear night and leave it under moonlight for several hours or overnight. Check conditions: if rain or heavy dew is expected, bring the stone indoors or place it on a dry surface behind glass. Full moon nights are favored in crystal tradition for a deeper reset. Moonlight carries no risk to the stone's color or structure.

## Dry Earth Burial

Bury amethyst in a small pot of dry indoor potting soil or sand for 24 hours. Dig a shallow hole, place the stone, cover it, and retrieve it the next day. Brush off any soil with a dry cloth. This grounding method is used in crystal tradition to release heavy or stagnant energy. Use indoor or bagged potting soil rather than garden soil, which may be damp.

## Breath and Visualization

Hold the amethyst in both hands, inhale slowly, and exhale steadily across the surface while focusing on releasing accumulated energy. Repeat two or three times. This method requires no materials and is used for a quick reset between sessions or when other methods are unavailable.

## Recharging After Cleansing

In most crystal traditions, cleansing and recharging are treated as separate steps. After cleansing, amethyst is commonly placed near a cluster of clear quartz, on a dry selenite slab, or in brief morning sunlight to restore its energy. ## How Often to Cleanse

Crystal traditions vary. A commonly suggested schedule is to cleanse amethyst when it first arrives, after any extended period of intense use, and monthly during regular display. There is no physical basis for a cleansing schedule — this is a personal practice decision. As a practical note, amethyst kept in dusty environments benefits from a simple rinse, while pieces worn daily in jewelry may be rinsed weekly with warm soapy water per GIA guidance.
