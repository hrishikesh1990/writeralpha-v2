# How to Cleanse and Recharge Botswana Agate

Botswana Agate can be cleansed with water, smoke, sound, moonlight, or dry earth. As a microcrystalline quartz (SiO2) with a Mohs hardness of 6.5–7, it is physically stable, water-tolerant, and not reactive to salt or mild soap. The safest and most popular method is a brief rinse under cool running water followed by gentle drying. Each method is covered below.

## Before You Start: What Botswana Agate Can Handle

Botswana Agate is one of the easier crystals to care for. Its silica structure is stable, not soluble in water, and not sensitive to brief exposure to sunlight. It accepts salt, water, smoke, and sound cleansing without physical risk. The only limitations are:

- Avoid ultrasonic cleaners and steam: banded agates can carry fine internal fractures along banding planes that thermal or vibrational stress may open over time.
- Prolonged exposure to strong sunlight may gradually affect any surface dye, but natural Botswana Agate's pale gray-pink palette is not photosensitive in normal use.
- Do not use bleach or harsh solvents, which can dull polished surfaces.

For the full analysis of water safety, see [can Botswana Agate go in water](/gemstones/botswana-agate/can-go-in-water). For a site-wide overview of cleansing methods and which stones require special care, see [how to cleanse crystals](/learn/how-to-cleanse-crystals).

## Cleansing Methods

### Running water

Hold Botswana Agate under cool or lukewarm running tap water for 30 to 60 seconds. Use your fingers or a soft cloth to wipe the surface gently if needed. Pat dry with a clean cloth and allow it to air-dry fully before storing. This is the simplest and most widely used method for physically stable stones.

Avoid very hot water: rapid temperature swings can stress fine internal fractures in banded stone.

### Mild soap wash

For accumulated grime on a well-worn piece, add a small drop of mild liquid soap (dish soap or hand soap) to warm water, soak briefly, scrub gently with a soft toothbrush to reach any carved details or settings, rinse thoroughly under running water, and dry completely. Do not soak in salt water long-term — extended salt exposure can work into micro-fractures and is unnecessary for a stone that rinses clean with plain water.

### Smoke cleansing

Pass Botswana Agate slowly through the smoke of burning herbs or incense for 30 to 60 seconds. Ensure all surfaces are exposed. This method carries no physical risk to the stone and is the preferred method when a ritual element is important or when water is not convenient. Ventilate the room.

### Sound

Place the stone near a singing bowl, bell, or tuning fork and let the vibration pass through it for one to two minutes. Sound cleansing is effective, requires no contact with any substance, and is particularly useful for a collection of stones cleansed at once. Botswana Agate's dense microcrystalline structure transmits vibration well.

### Moonlight

Set Botswana Agate on a dry windowsill or safe outdoor surface on a clear night and leave it for several hours or overnight. Check that dew or rain is not expected. Bring it inside before morning condensation forms. This is a popular reset method in crystal tradition that combines cleansing and recharging in one step.

### Dry earth burial

Bury the stone in a small pot of dry indoor sand or potting soil for 24 hours. Use indoor soil rather than garden soil, which may be damp. Brush off loose soil with a dry cloth when retrieved. This method is used in tradition to ground the stone's energy and clear accumulated influence.

## Recharging After Cleansing

Crystal tradition treats cleansing (clearing old energy) and recharging (restoring the stone's working quality) as two separate steps. After cleansing Botswana Agate, common recharging options include:

- **Morning sunlight:** 15 to 30 minutes in indirect or gentle morning sun. Botswana Agate's natural gray-pink palette is not photosensitive, so brief sun exposure is safe.
- **Clear quartz cluster:** Place the stone on or near a [clear quartz](/gemstones/clear-quartz) cluster overnight.
- **Selenite slab:** Rest it on a dry [selenite](/gemstones/selenite) charging plate for several hours.
- **Full moon:** Leave outdoors or on a windowsill on the night of the full moon.

For a full guide to recharging options, see [how to charge crystals](/learn/how-to-charge-crystals).

## How Often to Cleanse

Crystal traditions suggest cleansing when the stone arrives, after intensive use, and monthly during regular display. There is no physical basis for a schedule — this is personal practice. Botswana Agate does not accumulate harmful substances; the intervals are a matter of tradition and intention, not physical maintenance. A practical guideline: cleanse it whenever it feels appropriate or after periods of emotional intensity.

## Storage

Store Botswana Agate away from harder minerals (topaz, sapphire, diamond) that can scratch its polished surface. A soft pouch or padded box is sufficient. No humidity controls are needed — unlike iron-bearing minerals such as pyrite, chalcedony is not moisture-sensitive.
