# How to Cleanse Angel Aura Quartz Without Damaging It

The iridescent finish on Angel Aura Quartz is a thin vapor-deposited layer of platinum and silver bonded to natural quartz. The quartz substrate is durable; the coating is not. Salt, abrasive scrubbing, prolonged soaking, harsh chemicals, and ultrasonic cleaners all risk dulling or scratching the finish. Safe cleansing methods are smoke, sound, moonlight, selenite contact, visualization, and a brief gentle water rinse when physical cleaning is needed.

## Why the Coating Defines Your Cleansing Options

Angel Aura Quartz starts as clear quartz (Mohs 7, chemically stable) and is then coated in a vacuum chamber with vaporized metals — typically platinum and silver. The resulting rainbow finish is permanent in the sense that it cannot be rinsed off with plain water, but it is physically soft: platinum rates roughly 3.5–4 on the Mohs scale and silver 2.5–3. This means the coating scratches more easily than the quartz underneath and is vulnerable to grit, abrasive minerals, and rough contact.

Salt is a direct risk: even dry salt is abrasive at the grain level and hygroscopic — it draws ambient moisture, which can work between the coating and the quartz surface over time. Saltwater is doubly damaging. Ultrasonic cleaners vibrate abrasive particles against surfaces and can chip the film at edges and thin points. Steam cleaning introduces both heat and moisture under pressure.

## Safe Cleansing Methods

### Smoke

Hold the piece in smoke from burning herbs, incense, or a candle for 30 to 60 seconds, moving it slowly so all faces are exposed. This is the method least likely to cause any physical change to the coating and is widely used in crystal practice for this stone. Ventilate the room while doing this.

### Sound

Place Angel Aura Quartz near a singing bowl, bell, or tuning fork and allow the vibration to pass over it for one to two minutes. The stone needs no physical contact with anything during this process, which makes it ideal for pieces with especially fine or worn finishes.

### Moonlight

Set the piece on a dry surface — a windowsill or outdoor spot — on a clear night and leave it under moonlight for several hours or overnight. Check the forecast first: dew, rain, or high overnight humidity will expose the coating to prolonged moisture. Bring it indoors before morning condensation forms. In crystal tradition, a full moon is preferred for a complete reset.

### Selenite contact

Place the piece on a dry selenite slab or inside a selenite bowl for several hours. Selenite is widely used in crystal practice as a passive cleanser and does not require the piece to get wet, be touched, or be moved. This is a practical option for regular maintenance. Do not use a selenite bowl that retains moisture from prior use.

### Visualization or breath

Hold Angel Aura Quartz in both hands, take a slow breath in, and exhale steadily across the surface while focusing on releasing accumulated energy. Repeat two or three times. This requires no materials and poses no risk to the finish. It is useful for a quick reset between sessions.

### Gentle physical cleaning

When the surface needs physical cleaning — fingerprints, dust — use a soft, dry microfiber cloth or a cloth barely dampened with clean water. If soap is needed, one small drop of mild liquid soap diluted in lukewarm water, applied with a fingertip or very soft cloth, then rinsed immediately and patted dry, is generally safe. Do not soak the piece, scrub with anything gritty, or let it air-dry while still wet. Do not use perfume, household cleaners, or jewelry polishing solutions on the coated surface.

## Recharging After Cleansing

Cleansing and recharging are treated as separate steps in most crystal traditions. After cleansing, Angel Aura Quartz can be placed in indirect morning light for a brief period — avoid extended direct sunlight, as some sources indicate that prolonged UV exposure may affect the iridescent finish over time. Placing it near a clear quartz cluster or on a dry selenite slab is a lower-risk alternative. For a full guide to charging approaches, see [how to charge crystals](/learn/how-to-charge-crystals).

## How Often to Cleanse

There is no physical basis for a cleansing schedule — frequency is a matter of personal practice. A common guideline in crystal tradition is to cleanse when the piece arrives, after periods of heavy use, and monthly during routine display. Inspect the coating periodically: if edges or high-contact areas are showing dull patches or fine scratches, switch to display-only use and use only the non-contact cleansing methods going forward.
